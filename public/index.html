        About

        A local, searchable knowledge base of publicly disclosed HackerOne bug-bounty reports. It runs on your machine, needs no account, and exports to markdown your agents can read.

        The report list comes from the open reddelexc/hackerone-reports dataset. Report titles and write-ups belong to their authors and to the programs that disclosed them.

        Code is MIT. Data is credited, not licensed.
